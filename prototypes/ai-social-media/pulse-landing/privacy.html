Information we collect

• Account details, such as your name, email address and login information. • Brand Kit content you add, such as logos, colors, fonts, voice settings and characters. • Content you upload or create in Pulse, including prompts, images, videos and captions. • Details of the social accounts you connect, needed to publish for you. • Billing details. Card payments are handled by Stripe, and we do not store full card numbers. • Your IP address, which we use to work out your country for billing.

How we use information

We use your information to run Pulse, generate and edit content, publish posts to the accounts you connect, process payments, keep your account secure and support you. We do not sell your personal information.

Connected social accounts

When you connect Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn or Facebook, we receive the access needed to schedule and publish posts for you. You can disconnect an account at any time in Pulse or in the platform's own settings.

AI processing

To generate content, Pulse sends your prompts, Brand Kit and uploaded material to AI models, including Seedance 2.5 for video. This data is used to create your content. The Trends feature shows public content from Instagram and TikTok, collected by a third-party data service. This content is not linked to your account. [Whether AI providers keep or train on submitted data to be confirmed.]

Sharing with service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run Pulse. These include Stripe for payments, AI model providers for content generation, cloud hosting providers, and Apify for collecting public trend content. Each provider may use the information only to provide its service to us.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. When you close your account, we delete your content and personal information within [period], except where we must keep it for legal or billing reasons.

Cookies
