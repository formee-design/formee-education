Cancelling your plan

You can cancel your plan at any time in Settings. Your plan renews automatically until you cancel. After you cancel, your plan and credits stay available until the end of the period you paid for.

Changing your plan

Upgrades start right away. You pay the new plan price minus a credit for the unused part of your current plan, your billing date moves to the day you upgrade, and you get the new plan's full monthly credits. Downgrades start on your next billing date. You keep your current plan until then, and you can cancel a scheduled downgrade before it starts. Anything above the new plan's limits, such as video length, resolution, brands, team members and custom voices, is locked, not deleted.

Credits

Monthly credits that are not used expire at the end of each billing cycle. Credits you buy separately never expire, and your monthly credits are used first. If you cancel, credits you bought stay on your account and can be used when you subscribe again.

Failed generations

If a generation fails, the credits used are returned to your account automatically. You do not need to ask for this.

Refunds for payments

Payments are not refundable, except where the law requires it. If you think you were charged by mistake, email support@formeeholdings.com within 14 days and we will look into it.

Contact

Questions about billing or refunds can be sent to support@formeeholdings.com. Formee Holdings, 41 Boundary Rd, North Melbourne VIC 3051, Australia.
